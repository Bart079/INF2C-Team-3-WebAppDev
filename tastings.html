Welcome to the Tastings page!

There are a lot of amazing types of beans! We love to share beautiful beans and different types of roasts with you. The experience consists of tasting blindly a variety of different beans and roasts. The host wil serve you a cup and you will review them give them a score and feedback. At the end we will showcase the winner and the runner-ups.

We are exited for you to join one of our tastings. The tastings fill up fast. If you are not fast enough you will be put on the waiting list.
